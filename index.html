Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes — Kitchen Wizz is free to download and use. A Pro plan (from £4.99/month) unlocks extra features, and every new Pro subscription starts with a 14-day free trial, so you can try it before you pay for anything.

When is Android coming?

Kitchen Wizz is launching on iOS first, with Google Play in the works. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know the moment the Android version is ready.

How does recipe import work?

Paste in recipe text, snap a photo of a cookbook page or handwritten card, or drop in a link — Kitchen Wizz reads it and turns it into a clean, editable recipe card with ingredients, steps, and timing, saved to your own library.

What about my data and privacy?

Your recipes are yours. This website doesn't use cookies or trackers, and we only ever hear from you if you choose to email us via the waitlist link. See our Privacy Policy for details.
