Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This privacy policy covers the Kitchen Wizz marketing website at kitchenwizz.com. For information about how the Kitchen Wizz mobile app handles your recipes, account data, and in-app features, please see the Kitchen Wizz App Privacy Policy.

What this website collects

Nothing, automatically. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers, and it doesn't ask you to create an account or submit a form.

The waitlist link

The "Join the waitlist" button opens a pre-filled email in your own email app — it doesn't send anything on its own. If you choose to send that email, we receive whatever it contains (your email address and message) and use it only to let you know when Kitchen Wizz is available to download. We don't sell or share it with third parties, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Railway. Like most hosting providers, Railway's infrastructure may log basic technical data (such as IP address and request timing) for operating the service reliably and securely. We don't access this data for any other purpose.
