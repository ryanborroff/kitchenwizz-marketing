Kitchen Wizz App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Introduction

Kitchen Wizz is a recipe management and conversion app that helps you save recipes from anywhere, convert measurements between different units, and plan your meals. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Kitchen Wizz mobile app.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (encrypted). You can also sign up using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, in which case we receive the information you authorize us to access from those services.
  • Recipe Data: Any recipes you save, create, or import into the app, including ingredients, instructions, and notes.
  • Shopping Lists: Items you add to your shopping lists within the app.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as preferred measurement units, dietary restrictions, and other personalization options.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, such as features used and frequency of use, to improve our services.
  • Device Information: Basic device information (device type, operating system version) to ensure compatibility and provide support.

Speech Recognition Data

When you use voice features to add shopping items, speech recognition is processed on your device using Apple's native speech recognition framework. We do not store or transmit your voice recordings to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of the app (saving recipes, converting measurements, managing shopping lists)
  • Sync your data across devices when you're signed into your account
  • Personalize your experience based on your preferences
  • Improve the app's features and performance
  • Provide customer support when you request it
  • Communicate important updates about the service

Data Storage and Security

  • Account Data: Stored securely using Firebase Authentication and Firestore database
  • Recipe and Shopping List Data: Encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard security practices
  • Local Storage: Some data may be stored locally on your device for offline access
  • Data Retention: We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (such as Firebase for authentication and data storage, RevenueCat for subscription management)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

Third-Party Services

Kitchen Wizz integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase: For user authentication, data storage, and analytics
  • RevenueCat: For subscription and purchase management
  • Apple Speech Recognition: For voice input features (processed on-device)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of data collection (some features may be limited)
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at ryan.borroff@me.com

Children's Privacy

Kitchen Wizz is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the date at the top of this policy and, in some cases, by providing additional notice within the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: ryan.borroff@me.com

Apple App Store Specific Disclosures

Data Collection

  • Contact Info: Email address (for account creation and communication)
  • User Content: Recipes, shopping lists, and notes you create
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns for service improvement

Data Usage

  • App Functionality: To provide core app features
  • Analytics: To improve app performance and user experience
  • Personalization: To customize your app experience

Data Sharing

  • With Service Providers: Firebase (authentication, database), RevenueCat (subscriptions)
  • For Legal Compliance: When required by law

Tracking

  • No data is sold to third parties
